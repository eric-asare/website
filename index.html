About.

I am a Global PhD student in Computer Science at New York University (NYU), splitting my time between New York and Abu Dhabi, and a member of the SANAD Lab, advised by Sarah Nadi. I work in software engineering: I study how Python libraries use type annotations and how those annotations affect the developers who depend on them. I have also studied how tools like ChatGPT shape the way students learn to think computationally.

Before my PhD, I was a Junior Research Scientist at SANAD and completed a BS in Computer Science at NYU Abu Dhabi, where I received the NYU Founders' Day Award. I have also interned as a software engineer in big tech (Google) and at two startups (Qlub and Kehillah Global).
